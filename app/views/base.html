Frequently Asked Questions

Welcome to the interactive FAQs section. Click on any question to expand and discover details about AetherBlog.

AetherBlog is a next-generation publishing platform where human creativity meets AI collaboration. Powered by Flask, SQLAlchemy, and the Groq LLM API, it allows developers and creators to draft, manage, and translate posts with visual excellence.

Inside the post editor interface, expand the **AI Co-pilot sidebar** on the right side of the screen. You can select target writing tones (such as standard or friendly) and target languages (English, Hindi, Punjabi, Gujarati) to generate outline structures, draft summaries, or translate your text.

To keep your dashboard focused, AetherBlog filters homepage content, tags, category metrics, and featured widgets to display only articles authored by your user account. This provides a customized command center for every logged-in writer.

If you haven't uploaded a custom profile picture, AetherBlog automatically hashes your username to generate one of 5 sleek, futuristic default SVG themes (Cyberpunk Robot, Sci-Fi Astronaut, Retro Game controller, Code Bracket shield, or Silicon Chip).
